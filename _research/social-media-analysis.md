## What they do, as shown by their posts

- **Luxury weddings.** The bulk of the feed. Signature elements: floral overheads ("the biggest floral overhead to ever be done"), stage and entrance design, custom glass tables and furniture, petal walkways, cold sparks and cloud first dances with lighting partners.
- **Hindu and traditional ceremonies.** Haldi nights, vivaah sanskar, modern mandaps.
- **Micro weddings (2020 onwards).** Backyard and living-room transformations, sold as a package.
- **Corporate and state work.** Hyatt Lime (2020 and 2025), the re-opening of the Red House, the first sitting of Parliament in 2020.
- **Parties.** Milestone birthdays, for example a 65th at the Hyatt Regency.
- **Rentals.** Grass walls, glass tables, table pieces ("Ask us about our grass wall rentals").
- **Destination work.** A wedding in Paris, credited by a partner vendor.

## How the feed works

- Most recent posts are **collaboration posts** shared by photographers and planners (Awesome Photography, Luvo Weddings, Raj V Photography, Yaisa Tangwell, The Event Element, Events by Tricia) with Event Experts tagged for decor. The company's own posts are fewer and shorter.
- Captions consistently **credit the full vendor team**, which signals strong industry relationships: Hyatt Regency Trinidad, Laura Darwent Weddings, Lollabee Group, Premium Events Services, Lazaabs Lighting SFX, Valentine's Floral Designs, Safran Patisserie, Rink Films.
- Older posts (2020 to 2023) are the company's own photos with short captions and calls to action by phone.
- Hiring is done through flyers: Warehouse Supervisor (current), experienced event staff (2025).
- One client review was shared as a post in January 2020. There is no other testimonial content.

## Things to confirm with the client before anything goes public

- **Photo rights.** Most portfolio photos were taken by wedding photographers and several carry watermarks. Each needs the photographer's permission, and ideally the couple's.
- **Couples' names.** Names come from public posts the company was tagged in. Confirm the client is happy to show them.
- **Event years.** Years shown on the site are inferred from when posts were published, except where a post states the date.
- **Which event some photos belong to.** A few galleries group consecutive posts that appear to be the same event.
- **Logo.** The site uses a crop from a social post. Ask for the original artwork.
- **Rental stock, opening hours and prices** are not published on social media, so the site does not state any.
- **Sample data.** Booked dates and enquiries in the admin are labelled samples.
